Android trial and subscription details

Applies to Android test candidates with subscriptions enabled. Existing versions without subscriptions keep their current behavior. Configured store products do not mean a new build has been released or accepted.

Select “Sign in & start 14-day free trial” to check access with your Google account. A 14-day full-feature trial starts only if that account has no previous trial. One trial per account; reinstalling or changing devices does not reset it. The trial does not charge automatically.

Hong Kong reference prices: HK$48 per month or HK$480 per year, with the annual fee billed in full. Google Play shows the actual local price and billing period. Payment starts only after you confirm a purchase and renews automatically for the selected period. Manage or cancel renewal in Google Play; access continues through the paid period after cancellation. Refunds follow the applicable store rules.

Each person subscribes individually. A team owner’s payment does not subscribe team members. Use the original account on the same platform; cross-platform access is not promised. Restore purchases checks existing access. Verified earlier purchase rights must be preserved before migration.

Photos are stored on your device or in Google Drive / OneDrive storage provided by you or your team. Gonfolio does not provide cloud photo storage. Monthly and annual fees do not include cloud storage capacity.

After expiry you can still view, share, export and back up existing photos. New capture, editing, PDF creation, uploads and new team management actions require valid access. Existing photos, labels, cloud files and queued work remain. Offline access is limited to the original expiry or 24 hours after the last verification, whichever is earlier.

License testing must use an explicitly marked Google Play test payment method. Internal-track membership alone does not guarantee free purchases. Cancel if a real payment card is shown and check the test account.
